Our Story

Crafted with Passion,
Served with Soul

Brewed & Beyond started as a single espresso cart in 2012 — a dream to bring truly exceptional coffee to our neighborhood. Today, our cozy shop is a haven for coffee lovers, remote workers, and those who simply love a perfect cup.

We source our beans directly from ethical farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala — roasted in-house every morning so every sip is as fresh as it gets.
