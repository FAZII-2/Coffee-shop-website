Who We Are

Our Story

Born from a love of craft coffee and community — Brewed & Beyond has been a neighborhood staple since 2012.

From Cart to Community

A Cup, a Dream, and a Cart on the Corner

In 2012, Marco & Elena Cortez set up a small espresso cart outside their apartment building with one dream: to make coffee worth waking up for. Word spread quickly. The line grew. And before long, they turned that cart into a proper shop.

Today, Brewed & Beyond is more than a coffee shop — it's a daily ritual for hundreds of guests, a second office for remote workers, and the backdrop for countless first dates, late-night study sessions, and lazy Sunday mornings.

We source directly from small farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala, roasting every batch in-house each morning. Because coffee this good deserves to be fresh.

Our Journey

Built Year by Year

2012

The Espresso Cart

Marco & Elena launch a humble espresso cart outside their apartment. The line wraps around the block within a week.

2014

Our First Café

We sign a lease on 24 Caramel Lane and open our first proper café with 8 seats and a whole lot of heart.

2017

In-House Roastery

We install our own 12kg roaster and begin roasting single-origin beans fresh every morning.

2020

Through the Storm

We pivoted to takeaway-only and survived. Our community showed up for us, and we'll never forget it.

2024

Full Renovation & Expansion

A full shop renovation, 60 seats, a dedicated cold brew bar, and our first pastry kitchen.
