Frequently Asked

Walk-ins are always welcome! We reserve roughly half of our seating for walk-in guests. However, if you're coming with a group of 4 or more, we strongly recommend a reservation to guarantee your preferred time.

Yes! Free high-speed WiFi is available for all guests. The password is on your receipt or just ask any of our baristas. We love being your remote office on weekdays.

Absolutely! We offer private bookings for groups of 15–60 people on Monday–Wednesday evenings. This includes a custom menu, dedicated staff, and a beautiful setting. Email us at events@brewedandbeyond.com for details.

Yes! We stock oat milk, almond milk, coconut milk, and soy milk at no extra charge. Many of our pastries and drinks are also vegan — look for the 🌿 vegan badge on the menu.

We source single-origin beans directly from small family farms in Yirgacheffe (Ethiopia), Huila (Colombia), and Antigua (Guatemala). We visit our farm partners annually and pay a price well above the Fairtrade premium.

There is a public parking garage one block north on Blend Street (validated for 2 hours with any purchase). Street parking is available on Caramel Lane after 6pm on weekdays and all day on weekends. We're also right next to the 6 train stop at 28th St.
